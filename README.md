# MapAble Academy Business Plan

## Executive Summary

MapAble Academy is a dedicated e-learning Registered Training Organisation (RTO) that will build and upskill the disability support workforce. The Academy will first meet MapAble's internal workforce needs before expanding to external clients. NDIS providers will be able to contract MapAble Academy for staff training, and aspiring support workers will have access to accredited qualifications tailored to the disability sector. By combining MapAble's real-world service delivery experience with RTO capabilities, the Academy will deliver flexible, high-quality training that secures MapAble's staffing pipeline, generates revenue, and elevates the broader workforce.

## Company Overview

MapAble Academy operates as an arm of MapAble, integrating the existing DisAcademy training initiative into a cohesive e-learning platform under a single brand. As a certified RTO, the Academy delivers nationally recognized vocational qualifications and competency-based training. Its mission is to empower support workers with the skills and knowledge required for safe, person-centered disability support. In the startup phase, the Academy focuses on internal capacity building for MapAble employees. After demonstrating success internally, it transitions to external services for other NDIS providers and individuals seeking entry into the sector, ultimately becoming a reputable provider of disability support training.

## Market Analysis

### Industry Overview

Australia's disability services industry is rapidly expanding due to the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS), but the sector faces a critical workforce shortage. The NDIS Review estimates a need for approximately 128,000 additional disability support workers by mid-2025, while the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission reported a shortfall of around 120,000 workers last year. High turnover rates compound recruitment challenges—about 70% of providers report difficulty sourcing support workers, and many current workers are considering leaving the sector. These trends highlight an urgent need for effective workforce development solutions.

### Target Market Needs

- **MapAble's Internal Workforce:** Training internal recruits ensures new staff align with MapAble's service model and values, reduces recruitment costs, and improves retention by demonstrating investment in employee development.
- **NDIS Providers:** External providers, especially small and medium organizations, need cost-effective, compliant training solutions for their staff. MapAble Academy offers ready-made induction and professional development programs that meet NDIS Code of Conduct requirements.
- **Aspiring Support Workers:** Individuals entering the sector need formal qualifications like Certificate III in Individual Support (Disability) and Certificate IV in Disability. The Academy's e-learning approach increases accessibility for geographically dispersed or working learners.

### Competitive Landscape

Current options include public TAFEs, private RTOs, and in-house programs, along with free NDIS Commission modules. MapAble Academy differentiates itself through:

1. **Specialized Focus:** Exclusive emphasis on disability and NDIS-specific training co-designed with industry professionals and people with lived experience.
2. **Optimized E-Learning:** Engaging, accessible online delivery with interactive content, supporting learners across regions.
3. **Integrated Provider Services:** A comprehensive catalog combining accredited qualifications and tailored short courses for provider partners.
4. **Regulatory Emphasis:** Continuous alignment with NDIS Practice Standards and compliance requirements.

## Services and Offerings

1. **Accredited Courses:** Certificate III and IV qualifications delivered through interactive online modules with practical components coordinated via MapAble and partner providers.
2. **Short Courses & Workshops:** Non-accredited modules covering induction, behavior support, infection control, manual handling, person-centered active support, and more.
3. **Provider Contract Packages:** Customized onboarding bundles, annual training plans, and blended learning options (online modules, webinars, onsite workshops) for NDIS providers.
4. **Consulting & Custom Content:** Advisory services for training needs analysis, capability mapping, and bespoke module development.

All offerings are delivered via an accessible Learning Management System (LMS) supporting multimedia content, progress tracking, and certificates of completion.

## Strategy and Implementation

1. **Phase 1 – Establish & Pilot (Months 0–12):** Complete RTO registration, set up the LMS, integrate DisAcademy content, and pilot training with MapAble's staff to refine content and processes.
2. **Phase 2 – External Market Expansion (Months 12–24):** Market services to other NDIS providers, scale instructional capacity, and secure provider contracts while ensuring content remains up to date with NDIS standards.
3. **Phase 3 – Individual Enrollment & Sector Leadership (Year 3+):** Open courses to individuals, partner with employment services, publish thought leadership, and continuously evolve curriculum offerings.

## Operations and Management

- **Compliance:** Appoint an RTO Manager to oversee ASQA standards, trainer qualifications, assessment validation, and alignment with NDIS Practice Standards.
- **Content Development:** Combine instructional designers with MapAble subject matter experts to produce scenario-based, adult-learning-aligned modules.
- **Technology:** Maintain a mobile-friendly LMS with strong user support, data security, and integration options for provider HR systems.
- **Staffing:** Employ qualified trainers with sector experience, supported by administrative and client success teams; build partnerships for student placements.
- **Risk Management:** Address risks such as course dropout, regulatory changes, technology reliability, and competition through learner support, agile updates, robust infrastructure, and continuous innovation.

## Marketing and Sales Strategy

- **Brand Positioning:** Present MapAble Academy as a mission-driven partner that elevates disability support quality.
- **Digital Marketing:** Utilize a professional website, SEO, content marketing, and social media to reach both providers and individual learners.
- **Direct Outreach:** Leverage MapAble's networks, attend sector events, and offer demos to build trust with NDIS providers.
- **Partnerships & Referrals:** Encourage testimonials, collaborate with complementary training providers, and align with workforce initiatives.
- **Pricing:** Offer competitive pricing, flexible payment plans, and subscription models highlighting ROI through reduced onboarding time and compliance assurance.

## Financial Outlook

- **Initial Investment:** Fund LMS setup, content creation, and compliance through MapAble's internal budget, offsetting costs by replacing external training spend.
- **Revenue Streams:** Combine B2B provider contracts with B2C course fees; add consulting revenue as offerings mature.
- **Cost Structure:** Manage platform, staffing, content maintenance, marketing, and compliance expenses while leveraging e-learning scalability for healthy margins.
- **Profitability:** Expect internal focus to break even in Year 1, external revenues to offset costs in Year 2, and profitability with diversified offerings by Year 3.

## Rationale and Impact

MapAble Academy addresses the disability workforce shortage by producing well-trained support workers, improving service quality, and boosting worker retention through professional development. The Academy's e-learning innovation enables rapid, scalable training, especially for regional and remote providers. Strategically, it secures MapAble's staffing pipeline, creates new revenue streams, and positions the organization as both a service provider and an educator contributing to sector-wide capability building.

## Conclusion

MapAble Academy is poised to become a leading e-learning RTO for the disability sector. By following the phased implementation plan, maintaining rigorous compliance and quality standards, and delivering responsive, accessible training, the Academy will empower support workers, strengthen NDIS provider operations, and achieve sustainable growth. The venture creates a virtuous cycle—better-trained workers deliver improved outcomes for people with disability, enhancing provider reputation and attracting more individuals to this essential profession.
